# Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance

## Data Classification

All customer data is classified as either Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Personally identifiable information (PII) is always classified as Restricted and must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## Data Retention

Customer data is retained for the duration of an active contract plus 90 days, after which it is automatically purged unless a legal hold has been placed on the account.

## Data Subject Requests

Under GDPR, customers can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. These requests must be processed within 30 days. Submit any data subject request through the privacy@company.com inbox for tracking.

## Third-Party Processors

Any third-party vendor that processes customer data on our behalf must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before integration. The legal team maintains the current list of approved processors.
