# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require manager approval and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis depending on role requirements.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office stipend for remote employees. Requests for additional equipment go through the IT Service Desk portal.

## Core Hours

All employees, remote or in-office, must be available between 11 AM and 3 PM local time for meetings and collaboration. Outside of core hours, flexible scheduling is permitted as long as deliverables are met.

## Communication Expectations

Remote employees are expected to respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during working hours and to keep their calendar status updated to reflect availability.
