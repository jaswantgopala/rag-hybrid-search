# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses

Employees can be reimbursed for business travel, client meals, coworking space day passes, and approved software subscriptions under $50/month. All other purchases require pre-approval from a manager.

## Submission Process

Expenses must be submitted through the Expensify portal within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt attached. Expenses submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed except in extenuating circumstances.

## Approval Limits

Expenses under $100 are auto-approved once a manager confirms the category. Expenses over $500 require additional approval from a department head.

## Travel Booking

All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the company's travel portal to ensure duty-of-care tracking. Personal travel rewards points can still be earned on these bookings.
